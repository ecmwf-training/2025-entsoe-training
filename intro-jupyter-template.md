# Explore and Analyse PECDv4.2 Climate and Energy Data

This collection of training notebooks was developed for the **2025 ENTSO-E ECMWF training** on the **Copernicus PECDv4.2 dataset**. The data is provided via the [Copernicus Climate Data Store (CDS)](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/sis-energy-pecd?tab=overview) and combines:

- 🌍 **Climate data** (e.g. wind speed, global horizontal irradiance, temperature, precipitation)
- ⚡ **Energy indicators** (e.g. capacity factors for solar PV and wind power, hydropower inflows)

The notebooks demonstrate how to:

- 🔽 Download PECD data using the CDS API (full datasets and subsamples)
- 📂 Explore both gridded and aggregated data from **NetCDF** and **CSV** files respectively
- 📊 Generate figures, maps, and statistics for energy-climate analysis
- ⚙️ Compare different **wind and solar technologies**
- 🌡️ Analyze the impacts of **climate change** using CMIP6 projections
- 🗺️ Perform **custom spatial aggregations** using NUTS masks or custom groupings
- ☀️ Analyze **PV overplanting** scenarios

The notebooks are aimed at analysts, researchers, and practitioners working in energy planning, climate adaptation, or climate services.
